# MIDI Learn

**Put your MIDI controller in charge of LUNA.**

Touch a control, turn a knob, and it's mapped. MIDI Learn connects any MIDI controller to almost
anything in LUNA: faders, pans, mutes, solos, sends, plugin parameters, automation modes and
transport commands. No scripts, no setup files.

## Four ways to map

- **Mappings:** tie a knob, fader or button to one control in your session. They're saved with the
  session, so they come back every time you open it.
- **Channel Focus:** a channel strip on your controller that follows the selected track. Map the
  fader, pan, mute and sends once, and they work on every track.
- **Plugins:** map a plugin once, and your knobs control every instance of it, in every session.
  When that plugin is in focus its map takes over, and your other mappings come back when it isn't.
- **Click & Turn:** one knob that controls whatever you last clicked in LUNA.

## Benefits

- **Fast learning.** Press Learn, click a control, move a knob. Turn on Hold to map a whole bank of
  knobs in one pass.
- **Motorized controllers stay in sync.** With "Feedback" on, knobs and button lights follow LUNA
  through automation, mouse moves and session recall, without fighting your hand.
- **Knobs and buttons work your way.** Set a range or invert a knob; make a button fire on press or
  toggle like a latch.
- **Your layout goes everywhere.** Global mappings work in every session, and Templates save your
  whole controller setup to load into any session or move to another computer.

## Get started

Open the **Add Ons browser** in LUNA and select **MIDI Learn**. Requires LUNA 3.1.1 or later.
